# BSAH B2B School Partnership & Interscholastic Council (STPCI) Architecture

## 1. Institutional Pitch & Turnkey Integration Model

- **Capital Infrastructure:** Partner schools host or sponsor arena infrastructure while BSAH provides certified ex-military faculty (RVC/NCC Cavalry), bloodline Marwari steeds, and military-grade safety gear.

- **Interscholastic League UI:** The portal must feature a dedicated `/interscholastic` institutional dashboard for school principals and sports directors to track school ranks, tournament leaderboards, and inter-school tent-pegging standings.

## 2. Institutional Access Roles

- **Principal / Sports Director View:** Aggregate performance metrics, institutional insurance compliance status, and real-time attendance rosters.

- **RVC Faculty / Head Coach View:** Daily arena evaluation logs, jump height clearance tracking, and Sovereign Passport milestone validation.
