**Horse coat textures must be authored for the master's UVs.** None of the above photographs is a seamless albedo/normal/roughness set. Make a directional short-hair detail texture, region masks and a groom atlas from owned/licensed material or original authoring. Photograph projection requires removing scene lighting and validating seams; increasing the resolution of a blurry source does not create real strand detail.

## 5. What to take from the supplied tutorial

The [supplied transcript](references/flipnormals-transcript.txt) is source material, not an instruction file. Adapt these ideas:

- Establish scale and orientation first; settle large anatomy and the complete silhouette before pores or microdetail.
- Resolve form before deformation-friendly retopology, then project carefully and make UVs.
- Build material variation in editable layers and masks; assess it with the final lighting.
- Return repeatedly from render to sculpt, texture and groom; do not assume a linear pipeline produces a finished result.

Do not copy human pore patterns, human eyes, old Principled shader socket instructions, universal subsurface values, millions of runtime polygons, or offline-only hair directly into the website. Use the browser as the final material and animation judge.
