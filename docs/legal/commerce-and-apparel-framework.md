# BSAH Sovereign Cadet Equipment & Uniform Standard

## 1. Onboarding Cadet Kit (Mandatory ₹2,000 Package)

- **The Sovereign Passport:** Handcrafted obsidian leather passport with brass corner guards and gold foil embossing.

- **Academy Uniform Kit:** Custom riding breeches, certified safety helmet, equestrian gloves, leather chaps, and brass cadet crest badge.

## 2. Commercial Expansion & Apparel Line (Noir Heritage Theme)

- **Bespoke Outerwear & Accessories:** Linen riding shirts, tailored leather jackets, brushed brass bolo ties, and custom equestrian leather goods.

- **E-Commerce Integration:** Next.js `/store` route with dark-mode product showcases, 360-degree item previews, and automated UPI/Card checkout pipelines.
