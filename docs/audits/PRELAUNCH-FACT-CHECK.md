# BSAH pre-launch fact check

Audit date: 4 November 2026 (all "accessed" dates below). Scope: `lib/content/*`, section components, route metadata. Method: read the content, fetched the sources named below, compared claim to source, made minimal text edits. No design, layout, model, database or auth change.

**Source quality note.** Most sources fetched were Wikipedia (a tertiary source), the ITPF rulebook (primary for tent pegging), the AHA handout, Physiopedia and a WSU press release (via ScienceDaily). No peer-reviewed veterinary anatomy textbook or journal paper could be opened in this session (the PMC article returned a CAPTCHA). Anatomy and veterinary rows are therefore marked **HUMAN EXPERT REVIEW REQUIRED** where a textbook check is still owed. Nothing here claims external verification that was not done.

Status key: FIXED (text corrected) · VERIFIED (matches a fetched source) · QUALIFIED (already hedged correctly) · UNVERIFIED · OWNER (needs evidence from BSAH) · EXPERT (human expert review required).
Severity: C critical, H high, M medium, L low.
