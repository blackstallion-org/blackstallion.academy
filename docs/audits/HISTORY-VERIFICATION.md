## Cultural sensitivity

The site tells the Pratap story as legend and says it is retold "without reference to any one conflict, clan or faith". It does not claim a Mewar victory. Keep that framing. A Rajasthani cultural historian should read the Pratap, Rathore and Nachni passages before launch.
