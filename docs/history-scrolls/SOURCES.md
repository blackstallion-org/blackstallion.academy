# Sources and source gates

Each scene lists its sources on the page (`Sources and notes`). This file records what the copy may and may not claim,
and the questions still open. Copy was written from the standard published scholarship named below; quotations are not
used where the wording is uncertain, and no numbers, horse names or dialogue are invented.

## Open source gates (check before calling the copy final)

1. **JH01** — confirm the construction date of Jhansi fort (commonly 1613, Raja Bir Singh Deo of Orchha) against the
   ASI listing; the page says only "early seventeenth century".
2. **JH05** — length of the siege and the date of the storming (2–4 April 1858) against Rose's dispatches.
3. **JH07** — 17 or 18 June 1858; the page gives both.
4. **IN03** — the bargir/shiledar distinction as worded, against Gordon.
5. **IN04** — Palkhed (1728) "without a pitched battle" wording, against Gordon or Sardesai.
6. **IN06** — Silchar club founding year (1859) and "one of the oldest polo grounds" wording for Mapal Kangjeibung.
7. **IN07** — that the Hyderabad Lancers' role is omitted on purpose (escort duty); confirm unit list.
8. **WO06** — Marengo's arrival in England and death year (1831) against the National Army Museum.
9. **WO07** — "Army Veterinary Corps" naming (Royal from 1918) and Indian veterinary staff wording.
10. **AL04** — map coastlines are simplified outlines for orientation; route stops at published site coordinates.
