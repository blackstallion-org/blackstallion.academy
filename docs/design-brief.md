# Design brief

> Summary of the design brief in *BSAH Website — Design Brief & Build Plan* (Claude Docs). `CLAUDE.md` holds the enforceable rules; this file holds the intent.

## Big idea

**"An 800-year lineage, handed to your child."** Every BSAH cadet rides the horse Rajput warriors bred in the Thar. That lineage is the reason to trust the academy's discipline and to pay for it.

## Story: three acts

1. **The Legend** (heritage): the Marwari, desert-bred, battle-proven, nearly lost, revived.
2. **The Citadel** (discipline): ex-cavalry faculty, the creed, the 4 ranks, the 3-part assessment.
3. **The Ledger** (proof): passport, brass seal, verifiable credentials, live stall-cam, the numbers.

Public pages lean on acts 1–2. Transactional pages (`/verify`, `/pay`, `/admin`) live in act 3 and get quieter, more precise motion.

## Mood

Night in the desert, lamplight on brass: museum vitrine, field-marshal's map table, studbook registry. Not a "luxury gold" template.

- **Light:** one warm key light from above-left, deep shadow, dust caught in the beam.
- **Texture:** fine film grain, parchment, leather grain, brushed brass. Never glossy.
- **Pace:** slow, weighted, deliberate. Nothing bounces.
- **Avoid:** pure black, large gold fills, neon, glassmorphism, emoji-style icons, stock cowboy imagery, rounded SaaS cards, eyebrow labels on every section.

## Horse theme

- **The ears are the brand mark.** Marwari ears are lyre-shaped and curl inward until the tips touch (shared only with the Kathiawari). They become the monogram, the loader, and later the cursor and the audio "ear turn".
- **Hoofbeat rhythm** sets the tempo for staggered reveals.
- **Studbook language** for data: registry plates, microchip numbers in mono, pedigree trees.
- **Rajput miniature painting** is the historical visual layer (public-domain museum works).
- **Lances and the crest** from the STPCI medallion become glyphs and dividers.

Open question for the client: Marwari folklore treats a black horse as an omen of death, and the brand is the Black Stallion. Proposed framing for `/legend`: "they called the black horse an omen; we call it a vow."

## Signature moves

1. Dust reveal · 2. Rein pull (scroll-scrubbed scenes) · 3. Brass seal · 4. Ear turn · 5. Curtain. See `CLAUDE.md` for tokens and owning components.
